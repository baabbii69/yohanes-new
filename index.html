    ዮ Ge'ez, yo. Latin. Hover to switch.

    One mark, two alphabets.

    ዮ is “yo”, the first sound of Yohanes, written in Ge'ez, the script I grew up reading. Hover the mark and it turns into Latin. Same idea, two alphabets.

    That is how I work. I design interfaces in Figma, then I build them myself: motion-rich front ends and the Django systems behind them. Nothing gets lost between design and code.

    I have shipped enterprise platforms with teams, including two government systems I can't show here, and plenty on my own.
